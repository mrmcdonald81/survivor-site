How past champions played it

Leg-by-leg analysis of the 480 champion picks: average pick 75% to win; the #1 favourite only 31% of the time (top-2 48%, top-3 59%); their pick carried 27% of the field on average while the chalk carried 41%; only 5% of picks were under 60% to win. Chalk rate by season phase: 38% (legs 1–6), 40% (7–12), 17% (13+). Holiday legs: average 64% to win. Champions who owned several entries split them in 83% of legs where two or more were alive — and the extra entries went a rung or two down: average 72% to win (vs 80% for the primary), median #4 favourite, 38% of the time 10+ points below the primary, and a third of them on bottom-half future-value teams (e.g. 2025 Week 1: DEN on the main entry, ARI / JAX / PIT on the others).
