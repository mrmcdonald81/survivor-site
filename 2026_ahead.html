Look Ahead

A look ahead is a forecast of a forecast. Nothing on this tab is a pick, and the lines it prices do not exist yet — a future leg's number is this week's market rating widened for everything that can still happen between now and then (injuries, form, the line itself moving). Use it to see which weeks are thin, what a pick this week costs you later, and which teams to keep — not to commit. The week you are actually submitting is on This week's suggestions.
Every future leg is priced from the same market-implied ratings and Elo the rest of the site uses, widened by 0.32 points of spread per week of lookahead and tilted by each team's expected trajectory. The field is rolled forward through the fitted Circa choice model from Circa W2 selections PDF (graded): 8,601 entries alive now, spending teams every week — 120 simulated seasons of 6,000 entries, each season sharing one outcome per game so entries on the same team live and die together. Survivor counts are projections with a few percent of simulation error around them and are shown to two figures for that reason. Your assumptions live in this browser only.
