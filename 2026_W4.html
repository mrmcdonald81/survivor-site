How to read it: each row is a team’s remaining season, one cell per leg, numbers are its win probability there (horizon-widened — a December cell is a forecast of a line, not a line). Cost to burn is what taking the team this week knocks off your best remaining path — the board’s “Future cost” column, shown here beside the schedule it comes from. The brightest cell in a row is the best spot the team has left. Holiday spots matter out of proportion: none left are a separate contest week with a short team list, and running out of eligible teams for one is the most common way a live entry actually dies.
