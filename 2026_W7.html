How to read it: each row is a team’s remaining season, one cell per leg, numbers are its win probability there (horizon-widened — a December cell is a forecast of a line, not a line). Future value adds up how much better than a coin flip those spots are (only the part above 60% counts, because a 55% spot is not a survivor pick) — it is the same number the field model and the champion model use, and the teams at the top are the ones you want to still be holding in December. Cost to burn is what taking the team this week knocks off your best remaining path — the board’s “Future cost” column, shown here beside the schedule it comes from. The brightest cell in a row is the best spot the team has left. Holiday spots matter out of proportion: none left are a separate contest week with a short team list, and running out of eligible teams for one is the most common way a live entry actually dies. Free for = how many of your entries have not spent the team yet.
